I’m Sameer Panghal, a mechanical engineer focused on mechanical design, controls, robotics, and embedded systems. I like working end-to-end: designing and analyzing mechanical systems, modeling their dynamics, developing control strategies, and implementing them on microcontrollers and embedded hardware. I’ve worked on projects ranging from robotic and mechatronic systems to software tools for simulation and testing, and I’m especially interested in building reliable, real-world robots that bridge the gap between theory and hardware.

This site is a hub for my projects, notes, and experiments across robotics and mechatronics.
